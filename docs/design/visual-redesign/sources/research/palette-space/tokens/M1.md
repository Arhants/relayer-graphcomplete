# M1 Roadside Fog: tuned tokens (`engine.mjs` + `overrides/M1.json`)

Terms: ΔE is colour distance (about 2 is one hex step, 10 is clearly different). CVD means the same distance under simulated red-green colour blindness. Chroma is colourfulness (0 is grey).

1. **Roles.** Light is the default. Fog `#ECEBEA` (exact) is the page, sidebar and graph canvas. Pine-teal `#276459` (exact) is the primary button (white label) and the selection ring. Cornflower sky `#6087C2` (exact in both themes) is a quiet "attached / new" signal. Running is neutral ink. Failure, success and warning are derived at the same low chroma (0.11), so status stays as muted as the film. Dark has a neutral near-black `#100F0E`, primary `#007061` and ring `#42A594`.
2. **Changed: the dark "Accepted" green is lighter.** The candidate's own risk was that teal "selected" would read as "accepted". In dark, the engine's ring `#42A594` sat ΔE 5.6 from success `#60AC76`: same lightness, only 28° of hue apart. Success against warning was also only 6.4 under CVD. Dark success now sits at lightness 0.76 instead of 0.68 (`#79C58E`); hue and chroma are unchanged, and light is unchanged. Ring to success is now 11.3, and success to warning is 15.8 / 9.5 CVD. Shifting the success hue instead (152 to 140 or 128) was tried and rejected, because it pushed success into warning under CVD (4.4 / 2.8).
3. **Changed from the source colours.** Fog, pine and sky are used exactly. The dark primary is a slightly brighter teal than pine (chroma 0.089 against 0.065), because pine is only 2.8:1 on the dark page. The film mood survives: the neutrals are near-achromatic and the status colours are quiet.
4. **Quieter families were tried and rejected.** The six family colours at chroma 0.19 are loud against the fog chrome (the candidate's "pasted on" risk). A chroma cap of 0.14 drops distinctness to 10.3 / 5.4, below the floor, and 0.12 drops it to 6.7 / 3.6. The teal, sky and status hues leave too little hue room. The film mood has to come from the chrome, so the families stay loud.
5. **Key contrasts (light / dark).** Text 12.77 / 11.91. Primary label 6.88 / 6.02. Selection ring on the canvas 5.78 / 6.42. Edges 3.20 / 3.21. Failed outline 4.86 / 5.85. Label on the sky signal 5.15 / 5.24. Result: 0 failures and 2 warnings, both on the chart series (line 6).
6. **Family ΔE minima.** The six family colours are magenta, olive, purple, indigo, blue and crimson tabs. Light is 15.6 normal / 8.4 CVD and dark is 15.6 / 9.3, against 16.0 / 8.8 and 16.0 / 8.7 before. Both clear report 08's 15 / 8 targets. The chart series is 15.6 / 7.6 in light and 14.2 / 7.7 in dark, the same warnings as before (15.9 / 7.8 and 14.6 / 7.7).
7. **Known limits.** Dark teal links `#68C9B7` are only ΔE 5.3 from the new Accepted fill, but one is text and the other a pill with a check glyph. Crimson family F6 `#A2004E` is 11.1 from the quiet failure red `#9F4D49` but only 6.5 under CVD, so a crimson tab can look louder than failure. Failure always carries its octagon-x badge and caption. The source's dark cluster `#1F191D` is unused; the engine's dark page is ΔE 5.4 away from it.
8. **What only this palette does.** It is the only candidate with a green-teal interaction colour: pine at hue 179, where the nearest others are K12 at 207 and K5 at 214. It is also the only one whose single accent is a cool signal with no status meaning, and it sits on the most colourless ground of the set (fog, chroma 0.002).
