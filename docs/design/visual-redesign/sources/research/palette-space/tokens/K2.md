1. **Roles.** Paper `#FDFAEA` is the light page and graph (flat, with no grid) and warms every neutral (hue 98). Rose `#E65979` is the primary button, the selection ring and the running arc. Lime `#C1E357` appears only when GraphComplete accepts a turn, as a fill carrying ink text. Failure is a derived brick red, `#B23B19` / `#FE8160`, kept away from the rose. Focus and Stop are neutral ink.
2. **Source use.** All three source colours are used exactly, in 17 token slots, the most of the four Gen-1 anchors. The dark theme has no paper: brief B is light-first.
3. **What changed.** One override (`overrides/K2.json`): warning hue 70 instead of the engine's shared 75, as report 08 set it for B. Only the warning tokens move, each by less than ΔE 2 (colour distance: about 2 is one hex step). For example, the light solid goes `#BB7E00` → `#C17A00` and the dark solid `#CB8900` → `#D18500`.
4. **Result.** K2 now matches all 88 tokens in brief §6.2 exactly, and the family set is unchanged.
5. **Character.** Preserved. Rose keeps full chroma (0.176), and lime and paper are exact. Nothing was flattened.
6. **Key contrasts (light / dark).** Rose ring and running arc 3.30 on paper / 5.54: the tightest of the four, just over the 3.2 margin for thin lines. Primary label 4.99 / 5.54. White on the alt rose (Send) 4.55. Lime-fill label 11.84 / 13.13. Lime as a line (`#7A9500`) 3.27 on paper. Brick failed outline 5.67 / 7.78. Warning solid 3.07 on the field.

That is above report 08's targets (15 / 8) and above brief B (15.5/7.7 and 15.7/9.5). The nearest reserved colour is F1 ochre `#CFAF00` in dark, ΔE 10.5 from the warning.
8. **Watch.** In the source, the rose is ΔE 8.1 from signal red, which is why failure had to move to brick. A thin lime mark on paper must use `#7A9500`: the exact lime measures 1.39:1 there.
9. **Agent chart series.** 5 colours, smallest ΔE 16.8 / 16.7, CVD 9.6 / 9.9.
