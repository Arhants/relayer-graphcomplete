# M3 Olive Chilli: tuned tokens (`engine.mjs` + `overrides/M3.json`)

Terms: ΔE is colour distance (about 2 is one hex step, 10 is clearly different). CVD means the same distance under simulated red-green colour blindness. Chroma is colourfulness (0 is grey).

1. **Roles.** Light is the default. Sand `#E6DCC9` (exact) is the graph canvas, with registration crosses, under paler cream chrome. Olive drab `#464A32` (exact) is the primary button (white label) and the selection ring. Chilli `#C03636` (exact in both themes) means failure only: the failed outline, the badge and the solid. Deleted diff lines use muted text plus "−", so there is only one red. Running is neutral ink, and the families are whole tiles (Gen-1 D).
2. **Changed: the dark primary is olive drab again.** Olive drab is only 2.08:1 on the dark page. The engine therefore made it lighter, but it also raised its chroma from 0.039 to 0.113, which turned it into olive-oil `#5E6800` (ΔE 11.9 from the source). The dark primary is now `#62664D`, the same olive drab moved in lightness only (0.40 to 0.50). It reaches 3.21 / 3.06:1 on page and surface and carries a 5.95:1 white label.
3. **Kept on purpose.** Dark links `#B5BF77` and the dark ring `#909952` keep the engine's extra chroma (0.097). Olive drab at that lightness (`#B6BB9F`) would be only ΔE 3.0 from muted text `#BDB9B1`, which is exactly the candidate's "links read like body text" risk. Light keeps every source colour exactly, and dark now reads drab rather than lime.
4. **Key contrasts (light / dark).** Text 13.34 / 11.89. Primary label 9.19 / 5.95. Selection ring on the canvas 6.76 / 6.25. Edges 3.23 / 3.20. Failed outline 4.04 / 3.48. Failed-badge glyph 5.49 / 5.49. Muted caption on sand 4.53. Result: 0 failures and 2 warnings, both on the families (line 5).
5. **Family ΔE minima.** The six family tiles are pink, indigo, magenta, cyan, purple and blue. Light is 11.9 normal / 6.3 CVD and dark is 11.7 / 6.4, unchanged. That is above the engine floor (10 / 6) but below report 08's 15 / 8 targets. The olive (±30° of hue), the chilli (±30° for tiles), amber and green take the whole warm half of the wheel, which leaves only hues 172 to 355 for six families.
6. **Quieter tiles were tried and rejected.** A chroma cap of 0.15 fails at 9.6 / 5.5, so loud tiles are the price of six distinct families. The chart series is comfortable: 21.4 / 11.3 in light and 22.9 / 11.7 in dark.
7. **Known limits.** Pink family F1 `#C93A82` is only ΔE 10.7 from the chilli in light (31° of hue apart), so failure must always carry its badge and caption. The light alt button `#7D8927` (lime-olive) is 9.6 / 4.7 CVD from the amber "Needs approval" colour. The source's dark cluster `#19110F` is unused, but it is only ΔE 2.0 from the engine's dark page.
8. **What only this palette does.** It is the only candidate with no green or yellow family at all. All six node types are cool, from blue to pink (hue 222 to 354), against warm sand-and-drab chrome. That is the strongest warm-chrome, cool-content split of the set, and its one saturated warm colour is kept for failure.
