# K11 Linen Burgundy: tuned tokens (override: `overrides/K11.json`)

- **Roles.** Light-first. Linen `#EBE0D9` is the page, sidebar and canvas, used exactly. Burgundy `#640A2A` is the button, ring and link in light, also exact: 10.0:1 on linen, with a white label at 12.97. Red `#D83B36` means failure and is exact in both themes, with a white glyph at 4.57. Running is neutral ink. Families use gene D (the whole tile), with registration crosses.
- **What changed, and why.** One dark token. The plan separates "selected" (burgundy) from "failed" (red) by lightness alone, ΔE 27.3 in light. (ΔE is colour distance: about 2 is one hex step, 10 is clearly different.) In dark both derived text steps landed at the same lightness. The link `#F4A3B2` vs failure text `#FFA096` was ΔE 3.9, contrast 1.00, so a link would read as an error.
- **The fix.** Dark `accent-text` is lifted on its own hue (h7) to `#FFCBD4`, the first step ΔE ≥ 10 from failure text (11.0; 9.8 under simulated red-green colour blindness, "CVD"). It is 13.4:1 on the page. This is Gen-1 D's pale-ice dark link pattern, and K11 is "near D". D's link is ΔE 6.1 from its body text; K11's is 8.1.
- **Not taken.** Saturating the failure text instead (`#FF655A`) would have dropped its APCA score (an advisory readability measure) from Lc 65 to about 47. More family restarts gave mixed results (12.4 / 6.5 and 13.7 / 7.3) and cut the chart series to 4 colours.
- **Character.** Light is exactly the wine label: linen stock, burgundy script, one red seal. In dark, burgundy (1.62:1 on black) becomes a dusty-rose button `#A0455B` and a rose ring `#C57887`. The neutrals are almost grey, because linen's chroma of 0.015 halves to 0.0077.
- **Light contrasts.** Text 15.2 and muted 6.65 on linen. Edge and node stroke 3.22. Failure 3.52 on linen.
- **Dark contrasts.** Text 16.4. Button 3.18, with a white label at 6.01. Ring 5.84. Failure 4.19. Link 13.4.
- **Families (tiles).** Light 12.1 / 6.5 and dark 13.0 / 7.8. That passes the floor of 10 / 6 but misses report 08's 15 / 8 target, which stays a warning. Burgundy and red each block ±30° for tiles, so 337–57° is gone and four of the six families crowd into blue–violet. The weakest pairs are violet `#596AE8`/`#683BBB`, and blue `#005592` vs violet `#683BBB` under CVD.
- **Risks left open.** The comment-badge fill (`--accent-solid-alt` `#B95C71` / `#BB5E73`) is ΔE 9.0 / 9.1 from the failure red. Their glyph and corner differ. The dark button is ΔE 11.5 from failure.
- **The one thing only K11 does.** Its primary is the only one in the set that is both near-black (lightness 0.33) and clearly coloured (chroma 0.12). K6's oxblood reaches only 0.067, and M10's chestnut 0.050. So K11 is ink-led like Gen-1 D, but the ink is wine rather than black, and it takes exact button, ring and link duty at 10:1.
