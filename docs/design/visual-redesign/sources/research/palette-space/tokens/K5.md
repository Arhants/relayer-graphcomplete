# K5 Lido Coral (gene C, light-first): tuned summary

1. **Roles.** Off-white `#F6F9F8` is the light page, sidebar and canvas, and tints every neutral (hue 174). Teal `#048297` marks everything you can touch in both themes: the primary button, the selection ring and (darkened to `#006F80`) links. Coral `#DA6666` means failure only: the failed fill and outline in both themes, with `#C35153` under the white failed glyph. Running, focus and Stop are neutral ink.
2. **Source use.** All three creator colours are used exactly, in 15 token slots across both themes. In dark, teal and coral sit exactly on near-neutral greys (hue 174, chroma 0.007).
3. **What changed: nothing.** No check failed and there are no warnings, so there is no `overrides/K5.json`. A fresh engine run reproduced `K5.json` byte for byte.
4. **Character.** Preserved: the light theme is the poster's three chips as they are. I rejected the candidate's optional teal poster canvas. On a teal field the teal ring measures 1:1 and the coral failed outline 1.31:1, so both creator figures would have to be re-derived. The candidate's own brief is "on clean white".
5. **Key contrasts (light / dark).** White label on teal 4.52 / 4.52. Teal ring 4.27 / 4.22. Edges 3.22 / 3.21 and node outlines 3.22 / 3.79, against the 3.2 margin for lines of 2px or less. Coral failed outline 3.26 / 5.52. Canvas labels 16.3 / 16.5. Lowest muted text 4.56 / 7.02. Links 4.50 / 7.00.
6. **Families** (4px tab on the card). The smallest pairwise ΔE (colour distance: about 2 is one hex step, 10 is clearly different) is 19.9 light / 19.2 dark, and 10.4 / 10.3 under simulated red-green colour blindness (CVD). That is the best of K5–K8 and well above report 08's targets of 15 / 8. The nearest reserved colour is the pink F3 `#EA5DA6`, ΔE 10.2 from coral (floor 10).
7. **Competing states** (my check, not the engine's; the yardstick is the lowest value any Gen-1 prototype ships). Failed outline vs selection ring: ΔE 25.9, CVD 7.6. That CVD is below Gen-1's lowest of 10.5, because both are exact creator colours; the Failed badge and caption carry it. Danger vs warning solids: 15.1 / 12.3. Their texts: 12.0 / 9.9.
8. **Chart series** (agent authoring guidance): 5 colours, 16.2 / 10.3 light and 16.0 / 8.5 dark.
9. **Residual.** Failure is soft (coral chroma 0.146 vs signal red's 0.214), so a broken run reads more like a flag than an alarm. That is the source's choice, recorded in its risks.
10. **The one thing.** Among the light-first palettes whose accent means failure, it has the softest failure colour: coral at chroma 0.146, where K9, K11 and M3 run 0.17–0.20. It is used exactly, with every other creator colour, and with no override.
