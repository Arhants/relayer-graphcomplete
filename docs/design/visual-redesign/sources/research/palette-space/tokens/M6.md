# M6 Bone Umber: tuned tokens (`engine.mjs` + `overrides/M6.json`)

1. **Roles.** In light, bone `#E2D6CB` is the graph's poster canvas under warm-white chrome. Umber `#5F3D31` is the primary button and the selection ring, with white text, all exact. Rust `#9A543D` is a meaning-free highlight (`signal-*`), a "leather-bound book" accent for content. In dark, fawn `#AB8775` (an exact source cluster) is the primary and ring, with ink text. Failure is crimson `#D5305C` and approval is ochre. Running is neutral ink. Nodes are pills with solid family discs (gene B) and no grid.
2. **Changed: the dark primary.** Umber is too dark for 3:1 on black, so the engine lifted it to a rust at chroma (colourfulness) 0.12, `#9E4B2C`. That sat ΔE 2.3 from the rust highlight, so the button and the highlight became one colour. ΔE is colour distance, where about 2 is one hex step. It also only just cleared 3:1 (3.03). The dark primary is now the source's own fawn: 5.59 on the page, 5.88 for its label, and 13.8 from the highlight.
3. **Changed: failure is one crimson.** The derived dusty red `#9E4B54` / `#CC757C` sat ΔE 4.5 from the rust highlight in light and 4.6 from the ring in dark. Now `#D5305C`, oklch(0.58 0.20 11), is used exactly in both themes. It is 13.4 / 12.7 from the highlight and 24.1 / 17.7 from the ring. The candidate warned that rust "reads as a muted failure"; now failure is the only saturated colour.
4. **Changed: warning hue 75° → 85°.** This moves approval text away from the fawn dark link (ΔE 4.9 → 6.6).
5. **Character: preserved.** In light, bone, umber and rust are exact and nothing moved. The umber's low chroma (0.052) is kept, because it is the point: the chrome is leather and paper. In dark the palette is now fawn-on-charcoal instead of a second rust.
