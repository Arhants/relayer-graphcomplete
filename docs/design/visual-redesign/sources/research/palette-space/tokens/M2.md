# M2 Vanity Blush: tuned tokens (`engine.mjs` + `overrides/M2.json`)

Terms: ΔE is colour distance (about 2 is one hex step, 10 is clearly different). CVD means the same distance under simulated red-green colour blindness. Chroma is colourfulness (0 is grey).

1. **Roles.** Dark is the default. Cocoa-black `#181216` (exact) is the page and the graph canvas. Latte `#A3897C` (exact in both themes) is the primary button (dark label) and the dark selection ring. Blush `#DF927A` (exact in both themes) is the one warm glow and means live work; it is a fill and a mark, and the running arc itself stays neutral ink. Failure is a saturated red, and success and warning are derived at chroma 0.11. Light uses faint mauve neutrals seeded from the cocoa, because the source has no light neutral.
2. **Changed: the failure red is now clearly apart from the glow.** The candidate asked for "a derived red, kept clearly more saturated than the blush". The engine takes status chroma from the latte (0.11), so the dark failure colour `#E88D94` landed ΔE 4.4 (CVD 3.1) from the blush, and "failed" looked like "live". Failure is now chroma 0.19 at the engine's hue 15 and darker than the blush: `#BD1F44` in light and `#D23855` in dark. Failure against the glow is 16.2 / 11.8 CVD in light and 18.5 / 14.7 in dark.
3. **Changed: latte stays latte.** Report 08's rule gives every interaction colour at least chroma 0.12. That turned the latte into rust and peach, a second warm glow beside the blush: the light alt button was ΔE 7.2 from it and the dark link 6.9. These tokens go back to latte hue 48 at chroma 0.06 (the source is 0.037). Each sits at the nearest lightness that clears its contrast floor and stays ≥ 10 from the blush: light alt button and ring `#8C6450`, light link `#805945`, dark alt button `#926A56`, dark link `#F3C6AF`. The blush is now the only colourful colour in the chrome; chrome chroma peaks at 0.060, down from 0.120.
4. **Trade-off.** Latte links are close to muted text in colour (ΔE 5.9 in light) and only 2.8:1 against body text, so they must be underlined. Selected rows keep the brief's leading bar and weight 600.
5. **Key contrasts (light / dark).** Text 13.28 / 11.73. Primary label 5.30 / 5.65. Link on its worst surface 4.69 / 8.24. Selection ring on the canvas 4.87 / 5.65. Edges 3.23 / 3.22. Failed outline 5.73 / 3.90. Failed-badge glyph 6.10 / 4.73. Label on the glow 7.03 / 7.50. Result: 0 failures and 0 warnings.
6. **Family ΔE minima.** The families use the icon-stroke channel (gene A): magenta, cyan, olive, blue, indigo and pink. Light is 17.5 normal / 9.3 CVD and dark is 16.5 / 9.4, up from 15.6 / 8.5 and 15.6 / 8.3, because the new red freed hue room. The chart series is 20.3 / 12.5 in light and 20.6 / 11.6 in dark. Quieter families (a chroma cap of 0.15) would still pass at 13.7 / 7.4, but they would weaken the small icon strokes this channel relies on, so they stay at 0.19.
7. **Known limits.** Under CVD, the latte alt button and ring sit close to the failure red (2.0 in light), because red-green colour blindness sees both as dark brown. They still differ in shape: a ring, against a failed outline with an octagon-x badge. Dark failure text `#FF9EA6` is ΔE 8.2 from the blush (text against a fill). The cocoa support `#363233` and blush-pale `#E3B7A9` are unused.
8. **What only this palette does.** On screen, colour means "something is running here". The blush live-work glow is the only colourful colour in the chrome, and every interactive colour stays at latte chroma ≤ 0.06 in both themes. Among dark-first candidates, only M7 (its near-duplicate) has the same intent; M2 is the pinker and darker cocoa of the two. K8 and M4 give live work a colour too, but both are light-first.
