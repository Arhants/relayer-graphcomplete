# M8 Petrol Camel: tuned tokens (`tokens/M8.json`, overrides in `overrides/M8.json`)

Glossary: ΔE is colour distance (about 2 per hex step; 10 means clearly different). CVD is simulated red-green colour blindness. C is chroma (colourfulness) and L is lightness.

1. **Roles.**
   - Light: fog `#D5D5CF` is the graph canvas and petrol `#495C61` is the primary button and the selection ring, both exact source colours.
   - Both themes: camel `#D7A366` is the "needs approval" fill with dark text, exact.
   - Dark: the reel's petrol mid `#5B6F70` is the primary and its dusk blue `#789FB4` is the selection ring, both exact.
   - Running is neutral ink. Failure is the engine's muted brick red, `#9F4D48` light and `#CD7670` dark.
2. **Status.** 0 hard-check failures and 0 warnings, both before and after tuning.
3. **What the engine flattened.** Its report-08 rule lifts every interaction colour to C ≥ 0.12. That turned petrol (C 0.025) into cyan: the link became `#006375`, the comment badge `#008FA7`, the dark button `#006D80` and the dark ring `#3EA1B7`. The result read as generic app teal, not a foggy winter afternoon.
4. **Ramp change.** The petrol ramp now holds C 0.05, and only lightness moves:
   - link `#3A5F68` light, `#9AC0C2` dark;
   - tint `#E4F1F5` light, `#162324` dark;
   - comment badge `#5E8994` light, `#558386` dark.
   The dark button and ring are now the two exact source colours named above.
5. **Two separation fixes.**
   - **Hover stroke:** the light hover stroke (edge-strong) was ΔE 3.0 from the petrol ring, so it goes from `#5C5C58` to `#444440` (now 8.0).
   - **Warning:** "Failed" and "Needs approval" were ΔE 8.5 apart. The light warning solid moves from L 0.55 to 0.61 (`#976627` → `#AB793C`), which gives 12.3. It now takes a dark label, like the source's camel fill.

8. **Role-collision audit.** This is my check, not an engine floor. Its targets are no stricter than Gen-1's worst case. Misses went from 2 to 0.
9. **Left as is.**
   - The source's dark ground `#211D1E` is unused, because gene C keeps the dark canvas plain.
   - The family tabs use C up to 0.19 (a brief rule), so they are the loudest colour on the fog.
10. **What only M8 does.** It is the only palette that pairs a cool interaction ink with a warm approval colour on a neutral grey poster canvas. K12 has the same cool/warm split, but on a warm mocha sidebar. The light theme is driven by temperature, not saturation: a grey graph, blue-grey controls and one warm "needs you" colour.
