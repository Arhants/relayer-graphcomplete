# M9 Powder Lime: tuned tokens (`tokens/M9.json`, overrides in `overrides/M9.json`)

Glossary: ΔE is colour distance (about 2 per hex step; 10 means clearly different). CVD is simulated red-green colour blindness. C is chroma (colourfulness) and L is lightness.

1. **Roles.**
   - Light: powder `#D1D9E6` is the graph canvas, exact. The engine reads "B×C cross" as gene B: pill nodes, solid family discs and no grid.
   - Rosewood `#7F5853` is the primary and the light selection ring, exact. In dark the primary is `#805954`, one hex step lighter.
   - Lime `#B7C247` is the "accepted" stamp fill with dark text, exact in both themes. The light mark is `#717900`.
   - Running is neutral ink.
2. **Status.** 0 hard-check failures and 0 warnings, both before and after tuning.
3. **What the engine broke.** Lifting rosewood (C 0.053) to C 0.12 made it brick red, on the same hue as the derived danger red (27° against 25°).
   - Dark: the selection ring `#C67B72` was ΔE 1.5 from the failed outline, and the link `#F4A59B` was ΔE 1.6 from error text.
   - Light: the primary button was ΔE 6.1 from the failed badge.
4. **Ramp change.** The rosewood ramp holds its own C 0.053:
   - link `#7D5955` light, `#E3BFBA` dark;
   - comment badge `#A37974` light, `#99716B` dark;

   The dark ring is the exact source swatch `#A08079` (dusty rosewood).
5. **Danger and other fixes.**

That gives `#B63132` light and `#E8605B` dark, a failed badge of `#B63132` / `#CE4846` and error text of `#C33D3C` / `#FFA098`. 0.17 is the smallest chroma that clears every danger pair (I scanned 0.11–0.19), and red now means failure only.
