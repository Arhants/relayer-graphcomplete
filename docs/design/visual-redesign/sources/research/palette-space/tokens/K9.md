# K9 Riso Ultramarine: tuned tokens (no overrides needed)

- **Roles.** Light-first. The cream paper `#F5EACF` is the light page, sidebar and graph canvas, used exactly. Ultramarine `#1708E3` is the button, selection ring and link in light, also exact. Fluoro orange `#FF7543` means failure: it is exact in dark and exact as a labelled fill in light. Running is neutral ink. Families use gene C (a 4px colour tab on the card), with a dot grid.
- **Changed from the source, and why.** No overrides: the engine passes every check with no warnings. Its derived steps: light failure darkens the orange to burnt `#C04100`, because the source orange is only 2.23:1 on cream. The dark button lifts ultramarine to `#264AFF`, because the source is 2.18:1 on black. It keeps chroma (colourfulness) near 0.27, so the "electric" blue survives. The dark ring is `#5F8AFF`.
- **Character.** The poster holds. The graph sits on the exact cream paper with the exact ultramarine as its ink. Cards and node fills (`#F1EFEA`) are whiter and greyer than the paper, because report 08 caps neutral chroma at 0.016. Nodes therefore read as white cards laid on cream, which is Gen-1 B's paper pattern.
- **Light contrasts.** Text 15.8 and muted 6.03 on paper. Button 8.05, with a white label at 9.63. Ring 8.05 on the canvas. Edge and node stroke 3.20. Failure solid 4.39, with a white glyph at 5.25. Fluoro fill with an ink label 7.07.
- **Dark contrasts.** Text 16.5. Button `#264AFF` 3.19, with a white label at 5.99. Ring 5.98 and failure `#FF7543` 7.18 on the canvas. Edge 3.24.
- **Family colours.** ΔE is colour distance: about 2 is one hex step and 10 is clearly different. CVD means simulated red-green colour blindness. The smallest distances are 18.9 normal / 10.1 CVD in light and 19.5 / 10.4 in dark, which beats report 08's target of 15 / 8. The weakest pair is green `#385F00` vs olive `#8F8B00`. The lowest tab contrast on the card is 3.05.
- **Risk left open.** Failure orange `#C04100` and approval ochre `#986600` are ΔE 10.4 apart, but only 0.7 under CVD. The plan asked for approval to go "ochre-yellow". I tested warning hues 85 and 90: they add only 2–3 ΔE, CVD stays at 0.8, families fall to 16.5 / 8.7 and the chart series shrinks to 4 colours. I did not take them; the glyph and label carry the difference (brief §2.5).
- **Risk left open.** Ultramarine next to orange appears to sit at a different depth (chromostereopsis). This only happens where a selected node has also failed.
- **The one thing only K9 does.** Its interaction colour has the highest chroma of all 29 candidates (C 0.28; the next is 0.19). It is used exactly as the button, ring and link on paper, so the light theme is a literal two-ink risograph print.
